1. What KashLink is

KashLink is a piece of software. It turns an amount of cryptocurrency into a link that anyone holding it can claim. It supports:

  • NIM, the native currency of the Nimiq blockchain; and
  • USDT (Tether USD) issued on the Polygon blockchain.

KashLink is non-custodial. Every link is a freshly generated blockchain address whose private key is created in your browser or in Nimiq Pay and placed in the link itself. We do not receive, store, hold or have access to that key, to the funds at that address, or to your wallet. We cannot move, freeze, reverse or recover funds, and we cannot tell you who has opened a link.

KashLink does not hold balances, offer accounts, convert currencies or move funds on anyone's behalf. Every transfer is signed by a key that you or the link holder controls and is broadcast directly to the blockchain; the Service only prepares transactions and, for USDT, pays the network gas. Any fiat amounts shown in the app are indicative estimates from a third-party price source and are not an offer to exchange.

3. How a link works, and what that means for you

Whoever holds the link controls the money. A KashLink contains the private key to the funds. Anyone who obtains the link — by being sent it, by seeing it on a screen, by finding it in a chat backup, a clipboard, a browser history or a screenshot — can claim the funds, and you will have no recourse against us. Treat a KashLink exactly as you would treat cash.

In particular, you acknowledge and accept that:

  • There is no recovery. If a link is lost, deleted, sent to the wrong person, intercepted or claimed by someone other than the intended recipient, the funds cannot be recovered by us or by anyone else.
  • There is no identity check. The Service does not verify who opens or claims a link. A claim is valid if it is signed with the link's key, regardless of who is holding it.
  • The key lives on your device. The Service stores the keys of links you have created in your browser's or Nimiq Pay's local storage so that you can see them and take unclaimed funds back. If you clear site data, uninstall the app, lose the device or use a private/incognito window, those keys — and access to any unclaimed funds — are gone. We keep no copy.
  • Sharing channels are not ours. Links are usually shared through messaging apps. Their security, backups and retention are outside our control.
  • A recipient may bypass the app. Because the link is a private key, a recipient may import it into any compatible wallet and move the funds without using the Service.
  • Only trust the genuine app. Anyone can copy open-source software and host a lookalike that steals keys. We are not responsible for sites, apps or links that imitate KashLink. Check the address you are visiting, and only open links from people you know.

4. Fees

TokenFee

The USDT fee:

  • is paid by the sender on top of the amount, so the recipient receives the full round amount shown when the link was created;
  • is charged when the link is resolved — whether it is claimed by a recipient or returned to the sender. The fee covers the network gas that we pay on the sender's and recipient's behalf, which is spent in either case;
  • is shown on the review screen before you send, and again before you return a link;
  • is retained by us and is non-refundable once the link is funded.

The fee schedule is stated in the app at the time you create a link. We may change it for links created after the change. Network transaction fees on Nimiq or Polygon that you pay directly from your own wallet when funding a link are set by those networks, not by us.

5. USDT links and the relayer

A newly created Polygon address holds no POL to pay for gas, so a USDT link cannot move its own funds. To make claiming possible, the Service submits the claim on the holder's behalf through a relayer that we operate and that pays the gas. You acknowledge that:

  • the relayer is a best-efforts service with no guarantee of availability, speed or inclusion of any transaction in a block;
  • the relayer only relays transfers that its checks accept. It will refuse a request that would fail on-chain, that is below the minimum relayable amount, that does not match the expected transaction shape, or that comes from an address whose funds have been frozen by the token issuer;
  • if the relayer is unavailable, out of funds or discontinued, USDT links can still be swept by importing the link's key into any wallet that can pay Polygon gas, but the Service will not be able to complete the claim for you;
  • USDT is issued and controlled by Tether. Tether can freeze addresses and we have no influence over that.

6. Returning an unclaimed link

The Service lets a sender return the funds from a link that has not been claimed for 7 days or more. This is a convenience, not a guarantee, and it is not automatic: the return is signed with the key stored on the sender's device, so it happens only when the sender opens the app and taps Return. Funds are returned to a basic wallet address you control at the time of the return. For USDT links, the fee described in section 4 applies to a return.

Because the key is in the link, a recipient can still claim a link at any time before you return it, including after the 7-day mark.
